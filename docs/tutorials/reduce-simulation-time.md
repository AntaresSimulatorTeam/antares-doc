# Reduce your simulation time

There are several ways to manage reduce your simulation time
which are related to the topic of [reducing your output size](reduce-output.md).

**Reduce the optimization problem**​

- Reduce the number of nodes​
- Reduce the number of thermal clusters​
- Reduce the number of binding constraints​

**Reduce the number of optimization problems to solve**​

- Reduce number of MC years​
- Reduce the period of the year​

**Use adequacy mode, instead of economy​**

**Manage adequately the memory** ​

- Number of time series without refresh​

**Reduce the number of output files**​

- Year by year results only if necessary​
- Filter your results
